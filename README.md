# Browser Quiz

A group game of guessing popular, lesser known even some new web browsers that people use to access the internet, it doesn't give points so its up to you!

## Help

- Press `Next` and take a guess of the logo
- Then click `Show Answer` to so if your correct, play with yourself or as a group.
- You will learn who made them and when they were first released

Enjoy and have fun!
